# Release Risk Alerting System

## Background

Leaders and delivery teams needed earlier visibility of issues that could affect release performance.

## Problem

Critical issues and release risks were not always surfaced quickly enough, increasing the chance of late reaction and weaker operational control.

## What I built

I developed automated alerting logic and reporting support to identify critical issues and release risks sooner. The aim was to surface problems earlier, improve follow-up speed, and support faster intervention.

## Tools used

- Tableau
- Alteryx
- Supporting alerting and reporting workflows

## Users / scale

- Used by leadership and delivery stakeholders
- Supported release-monitoring activity
- Integrated into a live operational context

## Outcome

- Enabled earlier visibility of critical issues
- Improved speed of response to emerging release risks
- Supported stronger release control and decision-making

## What I learned

Alerting is valuable when it is both timely and trusted. The quality of the signal matters as much as the speed of delivery.

## What I would improve

I would add better threshold tuning, clearer escalation paths, and more historical analysis to understand which alerts are most predictive.
