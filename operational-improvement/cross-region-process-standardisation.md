# Cross-Region Process Standardisation

## Background

When teams work across different regions, inconsistent process handling can create friction, delay, and reporting ambiguity.

## Problem

Operational processes varied between teams and regions, making it harder to maintain shared visibility and consistent ways of working.

## What I built

I supported the standardisation of tooling and process handling across regions so that teams could work from a more consistent operational model. This included improving shared structures for tracking, communication, and follow-up.

## Tools used

- Power Platform
- Excel
- Reporting and workflow support materials

## Users / scale

- Applied across the UK, Europe, and North America
- Supported cross-functional and cross-region stakeholders
- Focused on improving consistency in live operations

## Outcome

- Improved consistency of process handling across regions
- Reduced ambiguity in how information was tracked and communicated
- Helped create a more scalable way of working

## What I learned

Standardisation is as much an adoption challenge as a tooling challenge. The best cross-region solutions are simple enough to scale and clear enough to use consistently.

## What I would improve

I would invest more in process documentation, regional feedback loops, and adoption reporting to spot divergence earlier.
