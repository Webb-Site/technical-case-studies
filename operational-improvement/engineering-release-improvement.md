# Engineering Release Improvement

## Background

Release performance needed improvement, and teams needed better visibility into critical issues, blockers, and release risks.

## Problem

The existing release process did not provide enough control, feedback, or visibility for stakeholders to act quickly and consistently. This contributed to weaker release performance and made it harder to respond to risks early.

## What I built

I supported improvements to release reporting, alerting, and process handling. This included analytics, automated issue surfacing, and administrative automations that strengthened release control and increased visibility for leadership and delivery teams.

## Tools used

- Tableau
- Alteryx
- Reporting and workflow automation tools

## Users / scale

- Used by engineering and leadership stakeholders
- Supported release-management activity in a fast-moving delivery environment
- Focused on improving live operational performance

## Outcome

- Improved engineering release rates from 42% to over 90%
- Enabled faster response to critical issues and release risks
- Increased internal capacity through more efficient process handling

## What I learned

This work reinforced the importance of combining reporting, process control, and proactive issue visibility. Strong performance improvement often comes from better operational feedback loops, not just better dashboards.

## What I would improve

I would build out clearer performance trend reporting, stronger leading indicators for release risk, and more reusable automation around issue escalation.
