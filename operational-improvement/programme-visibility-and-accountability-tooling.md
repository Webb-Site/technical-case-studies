# Programme Visibility and Accountability Tooling

## Background

Programme activity becomes harder to manage when ownership, status, and blockers are not visible enough across teams.

## Problem

Stakeholders needed clearer visibility into progress, responsibilities, and outstanding issues without relying on fragmented updates or manual chasing.

## What I built

I built and supported tooling and reporting designed to improve visibility of programme activity, make ownership clearer, and create a more structured basis for follow-up and accountability.

## Tools used

- Power Apps
- Power Automate
- Power BI
- Excel
- SharePoint / Dataverse

## Users / scale

- Used by programme and operational stakeholders
- Applied in live delivery settings
- Supported recurring follow-up and status management

## Outcome

- Improved clarity around ownership and progress
- Reduced manual follow-up effort
- Created a more accountable and transparent way of tracking work

## What I learned

Visibility and accountability improve when tools make follow-up easier rather than heavier. The strongest operational tools reduce friction while making ownership unavoidable.

## What I would improve

I would add clearer overdue action views, stronger reporting on recurring blockers, and more tailored views for different stakeholder groups.
