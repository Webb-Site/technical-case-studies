# Operational Workflow Automation Suite

## Background

A number of recurring operational tasks depended on repetitive manual handling, slowing teams down and introducing avoidable inconsistency.

## Problem

Manual workflows created delays, increased admin overhead, and made it harder to maintain reliable process handling at scale. Small inefficiencies repeated often enough to become significant.

## What I built

I developed a set of workflow automations and supporting tools to reduce repetitive handling, improve consistency, and create more structured operational processes. These automations were designed around real day-to-day team needs.

## Tools used

- Power Automate
- Power Apps
- Excel
- SharePoint / Dataverse
- SQL / Python where appropriate

## Users / scale

- Used across internal operational processes
- Designed to support repeatable workflows and reduce admin effort
- Benefited teams handling recurring process-heavy tasks

## Outcome

- Reduced manual handling across multiple workflows
- Improved consistency and process reliability
- Freed up time for higher-value work by automating repeatable operational steps

## What I learned

Operational automation creates the most value when it targets frequent, low-value manual work. The best solutions are often simple, reliable, and closely aligned to how people actually work.

## What I would improve

I would bring more of the automations under shared monitoring, add clearer audit reporting, and standardise design patterns for easier scaling and maintenance.
