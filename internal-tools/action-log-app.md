# Action Log App

## Background

Teams needed a more structured way to track features, actions, owners, and progress without relying on fragmented notes and ad hoc follow-up.

## Problem

Action tracking was time-consuming and inconsistent. Ownership could be unclear, progress was harder to monitor, and time was often lost chasing updates across different people and channels.

## What I built

I built an action-tracking application with list, detail, and edit views backed by workflow automation and supporting dashboard outputs. The solution was designed to improve transparency, accountability, and follow-up across ongoing work.

## Tools used

- Power Apps
- Power Automate
- SharePoint
- Power BI

## Users / scale

- Used by teams needing shared visibility of actions and ownership
- Designed for repeatable use in ongoing delivery and review cycles
- Supported live status tracking and process transparency

## Outcome

- Reduced time spent chasing action owners and updates
- Improved visibility of progress and blockers
- Created a more structured and accountable way to manage follow-up activity

## What I learned

This project reinforced how valuable simple, well-structured tools can be when they reduce ambiguity and improve ownership. Adoption matters just as much as functionality.

## What I would improve

I would add more configurable views, stronger notification logic, and clearer reporting on overdue actions and recurring blockers.
