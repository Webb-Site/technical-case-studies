# Global Change Management Rollout

## Background

As change-management tooling matured, it needed to support broader usage across regions and programmes with consistent ways of working.

## Problem

Rolling out a shared process across multiple teams and regions required more than just building a tool. It also meant improving consistency, supporting adoption, and making sure the process worked in real operational settings.

## What I built

I supported the rollout of change-management tooling and processes across multiple regions, helping standardise how change activity was captured, tracked, and communicated. This involved aligning tooling, process expectations, and stakeholder usage.

## Tools used

- Power Apps
- Power Automate
- Dataverse / SharePoint
- Excel
- Supporting guidance and reporting materials

## Users / scale

- Rolled out across the UK, Europe, and North America
- Supported multiple programme lines
- Used by a broad cross-functional stakeholder base

## Outcome

- Increased consistency in how change activity was handled across regions
- Improved visibility and accountability at a wider operational scale
- Helped embed a more standardised global way of working

## What I learned

Successful rollout depends on process clarity, stakeholder buy-in, and support for day-to-day use. Technical delivery is only part of the challenge when a tool needs to scale operationally.

## What I would improve

I would add more region-specific onboarding materials, adoption reporting, and feedback loops to identify friction points earlier.
