# Programme Risk and Status Reporting

## Background

Programme stakeholders need clear and timely visibility of risk, status, and progress to take action effectively.

## Problem

Without structured reporting, updates can become fragmented, overly manual, or difficult to interpret consistently across stakeholders.

## What I built

I developed reporting outputs and status views that helped stakeholders understand key risks, progress, and operational issues more clearly. The focus was on making updates more structured, evidence-based, and actionable.

## Tools used

- Power BI
- Excel
- SQL where relevant
- Supporting reporting and status logic

## Users / scale

- Used by programme and operational stakeholders
- Supported regular status reviews and decision-making activity
- Applied to improve clarity around progress and risk

## Outcome

- Improved stakeholder visibility of key risks and status
- Supported clearer and more consistent updates
- Helped make follow-up actions more data-driven and timely

## What I learned

Good status reporting is not just about presenting information. It is about reducing ambiguity and helping people act on the right issues sooner.

## What I would improve

I would add stronger trend reporting, more explicit action tracking, and better differentiation between leading indicators and confirmed issues.
