# Build Complexity Analytics

## Background

Stakeholders needed a faster and clearer way to understand build complexity, variant drivers, and the trade-offs between cost, weight, and engineering decisions.

## Problem

Existing analysis was too manual and too fragmented to support quick decision-making. Different inputs had to be pulled together before stakeholders could assess the impact of engineering choices in a consistent way.

## What I built

I developed decision-support analytics and reporting tools that combined queried and modelled data to help stakeholders analyse variants, feature families, and engineering change impacts across cost, weight, and complexity measures.

## Tools used

- SQL
- Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- Excel
- Python
- VBA

## Users / scale

- Used by engineering, finance, and programme stakeholders
- Supported complexity reduction and decision-making activity
- Built around recurring analytical and reporting needs

## Outcome

- Improved access to self-serve analysis
- Reduced reliance on manual investigation
- Enabled faster decisions around complexity, cost, and weight trade-offs
- Improved trust in outputs through validation, reconciliation, and metric standardisation

## What I learned

This work strengthened my ability to shape multi-source data into something decision-ready. It also reinforced how important validation logic is when stakeholders need to trust the numbers behind operational and engineering decisions.

## What I would improve

I would extend the solution with deeper scenario planning, more reusable modelling layers, and clearer monitoring around refresh and validation status.
