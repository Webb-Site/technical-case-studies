# Cost Weight Complexity Trade-Off Tool

## Background

Engineering and programme decisions often involve competing priorities across cost, weight, and complexity.

## Problem

Stakeholders needed a clearer way to compare trade-offs without piecing together separate views or relying on manual interpretation. The lack of a joined-up tool made decisions slower and less transparent.

## What I built

I built reporting and analytical views designed to support comparison of cost, weight, and complexity impacts in one place. The goal was to make trade-offs more visible and improve the quality and speed of stakeholder discussion.

## Tools used

- Power BI
- SQL
- DAX
- Power Query
- Excel

## Users / scale

- Used by stakeholders balancing engineering and programme trade-offs
- Supported structured decision-making conversations
- Applied within wider build-complexity and reporting activity

## Outcome

- Made trade-offs easier to compare and communicate
- Reduced manual effort needed to assemble multiple views
- Supported faster and more evidence-based decision-making

## What I learned

Decision-support tools are most valuable when they reduce friction in trade-off discussions. Presenting multiple dimensions together can significantly improve the quality of the conversation.

## What I would improve

I would add more scenario saving, clearer comparison baselines, and stronger summary views for leadership audiences.
