# Variant Driver Analysis Framework

## Background

Understanding what actually drives complexity requires more than reporting totals. Stakeholders needed clearer visibility into which variants, options, or part groupings were contributing most.

## Problem

Without a structured analytical framework, it was harder to isolate key drivers of complexity and compare them fairly across different contexts. Manual analysis made this slower and less repeatable.

## What I built

I developed analytical logic and reporting structures to identify and compare the main drivers of complexity across variants and related groupings. The framework was designed to make investigation more systematic and easier to interpret.

## Tools used

- SQL
- Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- Excel

## Users / scale

- Used by stakeholders exploring complexity drivers and reduction opportunities
- Supported recurring analytical reviews and structured investigation
- Applied within broader decision-support reporting

## Outcome

- Made it easier to identify the biggest contributors to complexity
- Reduced time spent on ad hoc investigation
- Improved consistency in how driver analysis was performed and discussed

## What I learned

A useful framework can save significant time when it turns repeated ad hoc investigation into something structured and comparable. Good analytical design improves both speed and confidence.

## What I would improve

I would add stronger drill-down capability, more scenario comparison views, and clearer explanations for less technical stakeholders.
