# Standardised Metrics and Refresh Workflow

## Background

Recurring reporting becomes difficult to trust when key metrics are calculated inconsistently or refresh processes rely too heavily on manual handling.

## Problem

Teams needed a more repeatable way to refresh, validate, and deliver reporting outputs while keeping metric definitions consistent over time.

## What I built

I helped standardise reporting metrics and supported repeatable refresh and validation workflows. The aim was to reduce variability, improve consistency, and make recurring analysis easier to run and maintain.

## Tools used

- Power BI
- Power Query
- SQL
- Excel
- Validation and refresh support logic

## Users / scale

- Supported recurring analytical and reporting delivery
- Used by teams depending on regular reporting outputs
- Designed to improve stability in ongoing reporting cycles

## Outcome

- Reduced manual effort in refresh and validation steps
- Improved consistency of metric definitions and outputs
- Made recurring reporting workflows more reliable and repeatable

## What I learned

Repeatability matters just as much as analytical depth in ongoing reporting work. Standardising metrics and refresh processes creates trust and saves time over the long term.

## What I would improve

I would add stronger process monitoring, more reusable documentation, and clearer failure handling for refresh issues.
