# Change Management Platform

## Background

Change-management activity across programmes was fragmented, manual, and difficult to track consistently across teams and regions.

## Problem

Teams needed a better way to capture, track, and progress changes without relying on disconnected spreadsheets, manual chasing, and inconsistent process handling. The existing process made ownership unclear and slowed down follow-up.

## What I built

I helped build and deploy Power Platform solutions that centralised how changes were captured, tracked, and progressed. The solution improved visibility, made ownership clearer, and reduced the effort needed to manage change activity day to day.

## Tools used

- Power Apps
- Power Automate
- Dataverse / SharePoint
- Excel

## Users / scale

- Around 150 daily users
- Used across the UK, Europe, and North America
- Supported multiple programme lines

## Outcome

- Reduced time spent on key change-management processes by 80%
- Improved visibility and accountability across teams
- Helped standardise how change activity was handled operationally

## What I learned

This project strengthened my ability to translate messy operational requirements into structured tools that people actually use. It also reinforced the value of designing around adoption, process clarity, and trust in operational data.

## What I would improve

Given more time, I would add stronger reporting around bottlenecks, more proactive alerting, and a clearer audit layer for tracking change progression over time.
